My Favorite Dishes

Here are five of my favorite foods and drinks, with simple ways to make them at home.

Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken adobo is a Filipino favorite simmered in a savory, tangy sauce with garlic, vinegar, and soy sauce. It is delicious served over warm rice.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Soy sauce and vinegar
  • Garlic, bay leaves, and peppercorns

Cooking Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken in soy sauce and crushed garlic for about 20 minutes.
  2. Put the chicken and marinade in a pot; add vinegar, bay leaves, peppercorns, and a little water.
  3. Bring to a boil without stirring, then cover and simmer until the chicken is tender and fully cooked.
  4. Uncover and simmer until the sauce reduces, then serve with rice.

Pinakbet

Description

Pinakbet is a hearty Filipino vegetable stew with a mix of colorful vegetables and a savory bagoong-seasoned broth.

Ingredients

  • Squash, eggplant, okra, and green beans
  • Tomato, onion, and garlic
  • Bagoong (fermented shrimp paste) and water

Cooking Instructions

  1. Saute garlic, onion, and tomato in a pot until softened.
  2. Add water and bagoong, then bring the broth to a gentle boil.
  3. Add squash first; after a few minutes, add the eggplant, okra, and green beans.
  4. Cover and simmer until the vegetables are tender but still hold their shape.

Fried Chicken

Description

Crispy fried chicken is crunchy on the outside and juicy inside. It is a comforting meal that goes well with rice or a favorite dipping sauce.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces
  • Flour, salt, and pepper
  • Cooking oil for frying

Cooking Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper, then coat each piece evenly in flour.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a deep pan over medium heat.
  3. Fry the chicken in batches, turning occasionally, until golden and cooked through.
  4. Check that the thickest part reaches 165°F (74°C), then drain and serve.

Iced Matcha Latte

Description

An iced matcha latte combines earthy green tea with creamy milk over ice for a refreshing drink.

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon matcha powder
  • 2 tablespoons warm water
  • 1 cup milk and ice

Preparation Instructions

  1. Sift the matcha powder into a cup to remove lumps.
  2. Whisk in warm water until the matcha is smooth and frothy.
  3. Fill a glass with ice and milk, then pour the matcha over it and stir.

Blueberry Lemonade

Description

Blueberry lemonade is a bright, fruity drink that balances tart lemon juice with sweet blueberries. Serve it chilled for a refreshing treat.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup blueberries
  • 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 3 cups cold water, plus sugar to taste

Preparation Instructions

  1. Mash or blend the blueberries with a little water, then strain if you prefer a smooth drink.
  2. Stir the blueberry juice together with lemon juice, cold water, and sugar.
  3. Chill, pour over ice, and garnish with lemon or mint if desired.
